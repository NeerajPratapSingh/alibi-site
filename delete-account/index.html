Delete your Alibi account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Deleting is permanent.

In the app

  1. Open Alibi and go to the Me tab.
  2. Under Your account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Want a copy first? Tap Download my data in the same place before deleting.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete the account for you within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Or write to hello@alibi.fit with the subject "Delete my Alibi account".

What gets deleted

Your profile, avatar and photos, check-ins with their photos, clips and locations, messages, votes, plan, food and workout logs, and settings. Nothing is kept.

Copies may stay in our providers' backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. Error reports are deleted with your account.
